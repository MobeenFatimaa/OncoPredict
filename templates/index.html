Clinical Practice Standards

Diagnostic Protocol & Risk Stratification

Standard operating guidelines for interpreting AI assessment outputs alongside pathology reports.

Standard: NCCN Oncology Protocols
01
Standard Baseline

Low Risk Tier (< 30%)

Model confidence indicates standard baseline risk. Pathological intervention is generally non-urgent absent physical symptoms.

  • Routine annual screening
  • Standard demographic tracking
02
Secondary Monitor

Moderate Risk Tier (30% - 70%)

Elevated probability requiring secondary evaluation. Indicates potential structural anomalies or strong familial predispositions.

  • Order targeted imaging (MRI/US)
  • 6-month follow-up assessment
03
Priority Action

High Risk Tier (> 70%)

Strong positive predictive model output. Prioritized clinical workflow routing recommended for diagnostic verification.

  • Immediate core needle biopsy
  • Multidisciplinary oncology review
